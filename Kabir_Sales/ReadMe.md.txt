#Kabir Retail Solutions

#Executive Summary
/> Sales show a mixed trend
/>In the year 2010, sales were at their highest and followed by a decline in 2011 up to 2012. This indicate inconsistency rather than steady growth. Therefore, sales are decreasing.
/>The overall business health is moderately healthy but unstable as strong performance potential is not being sustained year to year.


#Business Recommendations
/>From the dataset
/>No sales rep produces the two requirements.
/>However, Tola has the highest transaction counts i.e quantity sold and also generated the highest revenue.
/This suggest that: Tola is likely selling more items (either a lower or higher priced) and possibly giving more discounts.
Her strategy shows focus on volume and value, meaning she drives activity as well as maximum profit.

#In hiring Decision for 2013,
/>Total Quantity Sold:
2010 → 476
2011 → 380
2012 → 368
Revenue contribution also shows a drop after 2011.
/>In this regard,
It is recommended that the company should NOT hire more staff in 2013. Instead, it should optimize current staff performance.

#Reason being that,
/>Sales volume and revenue are declining, not increasing.
Hiring more staff during a downturn could increase costs without guaranteed returns.

/>Focus should be on:
Improving sales strategies,
Training underperforming sales reps, and
Increasing revenue per transaction.
